# Files

- [Instrument Verification](instrument-verification.md) - End-to-end flow for verifying and registering payment instruments (cards and Apple Pay tokens) through the Onecomm payment gateway.
- [Purchase Workflow](purchase.md) - Describes the purchase flow in PSP-Connector, including standard card payments and Apple Pay NAPAS transactions, covering validation, fraud checking, and provider delegation.
- [Refund Workflow](refund.md) - Describes the refund processing flows in PSP-Connector, including standard bank refunds, NAPAS transactions, manual overrides, and Apple Pay refunds.
- [Settlement Workflow](settlement.md) - Documents the settlement workflow within PSP-Connector, covering request validation, routing, and the integration with the OnecommPaymentServiceProvider for transaction status updates and refund processing.
